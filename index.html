We print one part at a time. Each one gets looked at before it leaves. No drop-shipped nonsense, no overseas middlemen — just good parts from our shop to your hands.

Ace 3D Printing Co. makes custom and ready-to-ship 3D-printed goods from our studio in Hunterdon County, NJ. We run Bambu Lab printers with PLA, PETG, and specialty filaments — dialed in for reliable first-layer adhesion and clean finishes.

Browse our Etsy for ready-to-ship items, or send a custom brief — we'll design, print, and ship it. Typical turnaround is 24-72 hours depending on complexity. Every part is inspected before it leaves the shop.

Need a prototype? A replacement part? A one-off gift? A run of 50? We handle it. No order is too small.
